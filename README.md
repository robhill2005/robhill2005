# Robert H. Puffer

### Silver Hill — Applied Data & AI

Senior data architect / data engineer building practical, governed data and AI platforms across institutional investments, public data, analytics, and workflow applications.

My current work focuses on a simple engineering principle: **strong data foundations first, semantic context and AI on top, with deterministic calculations and human gates around consequential actions.**

## Current projects

### SHARE — Silver Hill Asset Return Engine
Institutional investment-data and portfolio analytics platform. Security/reference data, market data, portfolio structures, benchmarks, exposures, deterministic investment calculations, semantic definitions, and governed AI-assisted analysis.

### RUDL — Richmond Urban Data Lab
A reproducible analytics platform built from official Richmond, Virginia public data. DuckDB + DuckLake, Bronze/Silver/Gold data products, neighborhood analytics, transit and housing/development data, and a governed natural-language question interface backed by validated evidence.

### SHIP — Silver Hill Investment Platform
Investment and market-data platform exploring portfolio analytics, research workflows, trading-system architecture, and practical AI-assisted investment tooling.

### SHORE — Silver Hill Outreach & Relationship Engine
A local relationship and opportunity-management application for organizations, contacts, meetings, opportunities, notes, and follow-ups, with explicit human approval around external actions.

### NextPath
An AI-assisted planning application for turning goals into structured plans, actions, routines, resources, and measurable progress.
